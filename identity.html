Section 02

Logo System

The AEL logo is a modular geometric mark. It is never redrawn, never reinterpreted, never stylized.

Logo Meaning

The logo is built from a 7x7 square grid. The top segment forms the letter "A" through negative space — a curved arch carved from a solid block. The bottom segment is a matrix of 4x4 blue squares representing structured intelligence, modular systems, and computational precision. The mark reads as both an architectural form and a digital signature.

Primary Logo

Dark background  ·  Blue background  ·  Light background

Safe Area

Safe area = 50% of logo height on all sides. Minimum size: 24px digital, 8mm print.

Correct Usage

DO
Use only the approved SVG. Maintain aspect ratio. Apply on black, white, or AEL Blue.
DO NOT
Stretch, rotate, or skew. Apply drop shadows or gradients. Change blue squares to another color.

Section 10

Protocol Summary

Architectural principles distilled from the full AEL Master Operating Protocol.

Core Disciplines

01
Architecture-First
Always begin with Architecture → Specification → Implementation → Validation.
02
Scope Lock
Define the scope. Do not expand into frameworks unless requested.
03
Single Source of Truth
Identify the authoritative source. Do not guess.
04
Minimal Safe Change
Inspect existing systems first. Implement with the smallest impact.
05
Reality & Verification
Distinguish Known, Verified, Inferred, Unknown.
06
Anti-Distraction
Answer directly. No praise. No alternatives. Stop when complete.

Final Execution Rule

Understand the System. Understand the Intent. Define the Scope. Identify the Source of Truth. Define the Architecture. Specify the Contract. Assess Change Impact. Preserve Existing State. Implement Precisely. Validate Completely. Report Reality Honestly. Recover Safely From Failure. Provide Complete Required Information. Avoid Unrequested Expansion. Confirm Completion. Stop When the Objective Is Complete.
